Corn is not tight. US 2026/27 stocks-to-use is 11.3%, the 42 percentile since 2007/08, after the October WASDE raised ending stocks by 282 million bushels (within USDA's own average miss of 331) ([WASDE](https://www.usda.gov/oce/commodity/wasde), agent 9).
Stocks at the start of September were 43.8% above their 5-year average; US export commitments are 9.1% below the same week of the prior three market years ([USDA export sales](https://apps.fas.usda.gov/esrquery/)); Conab puts Brazil's 26/27 corn at 148.02 Mt.
The next WASDE is on 2026-11-10. On WASDE days outside January, CBOT corn has been 1.314 times as volatile as the fortnight either side (paired t 5.2). That is size, not direction: the direction proxy since 2021 (-1.1193% per sd of the stocks revision) has no consensus behind it ([playbook](knowledge/ags/corn/events.md)).
Read: a comfortable balance with long-leaning funds (managed money at the 78.8462 percentile of three years).
What would change it: a WASDE stocks cut larger than USDA's usual miss, or export commitments catching up.
