# pm-agent-stack (personal build)

Research memory and monitors for a cross-asset PM, on public data. Steps 1 and 3a of the agent blueprint:

- **Research memory** — prices (commodities, US equities, Treasury futures, USD), EIA inventories, CFTC positioning, FRED rates/credit/USD and central-bank text in Parquet (queried with DuckDB), plus notes, podcast summaries and a trade journal in markdown.
- **Monitor 3a** — commodity supply-demand, US equities, US rates, USD and central-bank text. Writes a fact pack with every figure linked to its source, and ranked alerts.
- **Decision journal + sizing assistant** — `/journal` captures a paper idea in a minute; every idea gets a suggested size (Kelly-relative risk units, capped, stepped, cut for overlap and drawdown) and flags for under- and over-sizing. Evidence: `docs/research/sizing.md`.
- **Critic** (agent 5) — every new idea gets a card computed by code (market base rate for its target/stop/horizon, similar past ideas, trend, CFTC crowding, 2y-yield direction, the day's alerts, Zweig rule triggers) and at most three questions: a pre-mortem by default, a counter-argument or rule question only when the data gives a reason. Answers live in the entry. Fridays 18:17 BRT: "what breaks this trade" for ideas where something changed (`skills/critic-weekly/`). Evidence: `docs/research/critic.md`.
- **Consensus and narrative monitor** (agent 7) — a "Consensus map" in the daily fact pack: 18 news narratives scored as a share of all GDELT coverage (spreading, peaking, fading, steady, dormant), a trend-follower proxy and CFTC managed-money percentile per market, a label per market and a placement per open idea (with the crowd, against it, quiet, mixed; all inference). The morning note mentions only stage changes and his ideas; the critic card carries the crowd facts. Descriptive only until the quant gate. Evidence: `docs/research/consensus.md`.
- **Risk and crowding** (agent 8) — a "Risk and crowding" section in the daily fact pack, all in paper R: a risk table per open idea (R now, stop in daily sd, 1-sd day in R, share of stress risk, cluster, CFTC crowding on its side, flags), effective independent ideas, stress P&L on history back to 1986 (futures from ~2000, spot/index proxies before): a 32-episode library replayed day by day, the worst 1/5/20/60-day windows for today's book, 9 named one-month shocks under stress, 2008 and 2022 correlations, and the worst case at k = 3 (stops honoured and gapped), CTA trigger levels, and warnings (amber drawdown, the sizing cut counting open marks, clustered losses, persistent turbulence). `python -m pmstack risk` prints the stress report on demand. Descriptive only. Evidence: `docs/research/risk.md`.
- **Weekly review** (agent 6) — every Friday the scorecard puts process first (invalidation, P(win), sizing by the rule, critic answered, exits tagged), then leaks against his own baseline (held past invalidation, tilt after big closes, overtrading, exits by trigger), then outcomes gated by sample size with intervals and shrinkage, and one if-then goal for next week that code tracks. Evidence: `docs/research/weekly-review.md`.
- **Research digester** (agent 10) — `/digest <YouTube link | file | paper>`: captions or text stored locally, claims extracted window by window into **claim cards** (speaker, timestamp, verbatim quote, data/forecast/opinion, horizon, hedges), each checked by code: quote found in the transcript, numbers inside the quote, imported names and dropped hedges flagged, stated levels vs the stored close. The note is written in his summary format from checked cards and audited. Forecasts go to a **call ledger** scored on stored prices (no look-ahead); a speaker's record counts from 15 resolved calls, before that the prior is 47.4% (CXO). The critic card shows cards for and against an idea's market. Index: `notes/index.md`. Evidence: `docs/research/digester.md`.
- **Balance keeper** (agent 9) — a "Balances" section in the daily fact pack: US oil stocks against the same week of the prior five years (EIA's convention: level, range, z-score, change vs the 5-year change, days of supply), 4-week implied demand, runs and net imports, the crude adjustment as a data-quality flag; corn, soybeans, wheat and sugar from WASDE as first published (stocks-to-use, its percentile since 2007/08, revision vs the previous report beside USDA's own average miss, old crop beside new, world and world ex-China); NASS crop condition and quarterly stocks. Every number is used only from the day it was public. Descriptive only: no forecasts, never "vs consensus". `/balance` answers questions. Evidence: `docs/research/balance.md`.
- **Own test set** — 20 frozen tasks with known answers, graded by code, run three times before any agent, skill or model change (`/evals`); plus the digester's quote-check calibration and a re-digest noise study. Evidence: `docs/research/test-set.md`.
- **Network and reputation** (agent 11) — `/network`: post-call debrief (note, dated follow-ups from every promise, drafted recap he sends), meeting prep, follow-ups due, weekly dormant-tie reconnects, a monthly network page (bridging share, concentration, follow-ups on time, give:ask) and a monthly public commodity post (≤ 300 words + one chart from public-domain data, scored calls). Contacts and call notes live in a **separate private repo** (`../pm-network`), never here; every call note carries an MNPI attestation. Evidence: `docs/research/network.md`.
- **Research loop** (agent 12) — `/research`: an idea, a paper or one of the agents' own flags is registered first (frozen by hash, with the drafting model's recall guess), then tested by code on public data: the flag recomputed point in time with the agents' own functions, base rate and lift on independent episodes, a circular-shift placebo, Benjamini–Hochberg across every study ever run, chronological halves and drop-one-year checks, shrunk and decay-haircut effects; strategies against the long-only and historical-mean books after costs, with a deflated Sharpe over every variant and the post-publication period apart. Verdict + graveyard in `research/index.md`; the Risk section and the critic card show each flag's measured verdict. First jobs: the 16 agent 7 and 8 flags and time-series momentum. Evidence: `docs/research/research-loop.md`.
- **Fundamentals pillar: specialists** (agent 12 "Research", from 24 Oct 2026). `/ask energy: <question>` routes the question in code (`config/agents.yaml`) to a read-only specialist. Code builds a numbered input pack of every figure the specialist may cite (balance vs the 5-year norm, EIA report reaction base rates since 2001, CFTC and CTA levels, news narratives, STEO vintages, JODI, ANP Brazil, US rigs, front curve once stored). The specialist writes a **state card** (one JSON contract at every level), and code validates it: the schema, no look-ahead, and every number in the text tied to a cited figure. The chat answer then passes the number auditor. Knowledge lives in files by commodity (`knowledge/<complex>/<commodity>/`: primer, drivers with size provenance literature / own estimate / Bernardo prior, balance template, report playbooks, analogues, lessons, open questions). Memory is point in time (`pmstack/facts.py`: facts superseded, never overwritten, read `as_of` any date). Bernardo's studies enter through `knowledge/inbox/` (broker and fund material refused). Energy is live (S1); Metals, Ags, Macro context and the orchestrator follow. A 20-question energy test set is graded by code (`pmstack cevals`). Evidence: `docs/research/specialists-architecture.md`, `docs/research/energy.md`.
- **Quant agent** (agent 13, from 24 Oct 2026). After the US close, code builds the tape (`briefs/<date>/quant.md`). It covers trend legs and CTA flip levels: what flipped today and what is stretched against its own history since 2000. It adds realised vol regime and the implied-minus-realised gap (OVX, GVZ, VIX), correlation against a 3-year band, CFTC positioning on two clocks with open-interest growth, a half-month seasonal norm, and front curve and carry. A routine at 17:15 New York time (inside the CME halt) starts the workflow and writes an audited tape card every weekday (`/tape` on demand). Descriptive only; the testing gate's verdicts sit beside the flags. Evidence: `docs/research/quant.md`.
- **Number auditor** — `python -m pmstack audit <file>` checks every figure, date and alert ref in a written output against the fact pack and the journal; the morning note fixes problems once and flags the rest at the top.
- **Morning note** — a scheduled Claude task reads the fact pack at 06:18 BRT on weekdays and writes a Docs page (see `skills/morning-note/SKILL.md`).

Design: architecture doc in the Claude Project "Future of Asset Management and Trading". Rules for agents: `CLAUDE.md`.

## Daily flow
1. GitHub Actions, 05:40 BRT Mon–Fri (`.github/workflows/daily.yml`): tests → `python -m pmstack all` → commit `data/`, `briefs/`, `logs/`.
2. Claude scheduled task, 06:18 BRT: reads `briefs/<today>/facts.md`, writes the note, audits its figures, saves `briefs/<today>/note.md`, logs and pushes.
3. Fridays 18:17 BRT, Claude scheduled task "Friday review": `critic weekly` + `review weekly`, writes `briefs/<today>/critic-weekly.md` (critic pass, scorecard, debrief, next week's goal) and a Docs page.

## Setup
1. Repo secrets (Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions): `EIA_API_KEY` (free, https://www.eia.gov/opendata/register.php) and optionally `FRED_API_KEY` (free; without it the public CSV endpoint is used).
2. Run the workflow once by hand (Actions → daily-fetch-and-monitor → Run workflow). The first run backfills history (EIA from 2018, CFTC from 2020, prices from 2023). CFTC 2006–2019 was fetched once for the research loop (19 Oct 2026).

## Swapping a data source
Write `pmstack/adapters/<name>.py` with `KIND` and `fetch(cfg, since)` returning the columns in `pmstack/store.py`, register it in `pmstack/adapters/__init__.py`, and point the source block in `config/sources.yaml` at it. The monitor does not change.

## Known gaps (free data)
Front-month futures only (no full curves); no shipping or weather yet; Yahoo prices are unofficial and personal-use only.
