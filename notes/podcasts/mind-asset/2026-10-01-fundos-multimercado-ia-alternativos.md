## Key messages
- The industry is splitting into **specialist pods** and **platforms**; multi-strategy platforms keep outgrowing single-manager houses. Itaú positions itself as the platform.
- Alpha and beta have been separating for 15+ years; alpha now needs ever more **specialisation**. Recruiting focus: **single pods** (one geography or asset class) plugged into the platform's AI infrastructure.
- A structured, end-to-end AI project (about a year old): real-time data, news and research → an economist agent builds a country's macro view → portfolio agents trade it under a defined philosophy, supervised by people. Now **200+ agents**; Australia and Canada live, the US and Brazil in testing.
- **"Gestão ciborgue"**: three desks already run on the AI platform. The human governs the machine, partly because regulation requires a responsible manager.
- The main gain is **measurability**: each link in the chain is scored (inflation agent → chief-economist agent → portfolio agents → trades over a set horizon).
- Being standardised into agents: portfolio construction, regime analysis, asset pricing, econometrics, curve analysis, risk. Stays human: deep knowledge of the underlying asset, experience, time and dedication. Specialisation replaces the generalist "visionary" view.
