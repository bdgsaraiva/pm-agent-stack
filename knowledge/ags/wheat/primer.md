# Primer: wheat

This file holds method and sources only. Live levels come from the pack.

## Contracts and calendar
- **CBOT wheat (ZW=F)**: soft red winter (SRW), US cents per bushel. Yahoo continuous front month (personal use only). Kansas City HRW and Minneapolis spring wheat are not stored; the Pink Sheet's long-run series is US HRW.
- US marketing year June-May. Winter wheat is planted in the autumn and harvested June-July; spring wheat is planted April-May and harvested August-September. Condition ratings run for both.
- Wheat is a world market: Russia, the EU, Canada, Australia, the US, Argentina and Ukraine compete for exports (general knowledge). The Black Sea war risk sits in the narrative pillar (agent 7).
- No CFTC series for wheat is stored (the shared CFTC block is the notes track's file): an open question.
