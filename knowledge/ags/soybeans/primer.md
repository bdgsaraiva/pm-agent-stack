# Primer: soybeans

This file holds method and sources only. Live levels come from the pack.

## Contracts and calendar
- **CBOT soybeans (ZS=F)**: US cents per bushel, 5,000 bu. Yahoo continuous front month (personal use only). Products (meal ZM, oil ZL) and the crush margin are not stored.
- US marketing year September-August; the key US weather window is August (pod fill). Brazil (the largest exporter) plants September-December and harvests January-April; Argentina a little later. US export demand moves to Brazil from February.
- China buys about 60% of world soybean imports (general knowledge, not page-checked): its purchases show in the export-sales destinations (not split by country here).

## Balance (agent 9 and S4, in the pack)

- Conab's soybean survey as first published (`conab:soja`), revision vs the previous survey. USDA has run above Conab on Brazilian soybeans since 2018/19 (farmdoc daily 2024).
- ENSO: El Niño has tended to raise global soybean yields (Iizumi et al. 2014); the ONI is in the pack as context.
